I am early in my engineering journey — and I am using that stage to build as much as I learn.

I'm Dhruv Godhaniya, a first-year B.Tech Robotics & Automation student at Government Engineering College Rajkot (GTU). I come from Porbandar, Gujarat, and I am currently in Rajkot for my studies.

My strongest current interest is web development. I enjoy turning an idea into an interface that people can actually use, and I am interested in building websites and digital products for clients. Alongside web development, I am covering AI/ML concepts and learning the engineering foundations that I want to use in future robotics and intelligent-system work.

I do not see these interests as separate paths. Web development teaches me how to design useful products; robotics teaches me systems thinking; AI/ML gives me another way to make those systems smarter.
