PROJECT OVERVIEW

A practical control panel for hotel workflows.

The live application combines customer accounts, prepaid meal passes, a current menu, purchase records, custom charges and data backup. It uses IndexedDB for structured browser-local data and supports export/import of the stored records.

HIGHLIGHTS

Operational modules

Meal passes

Create prepaid meal credits with customer details, price, meals and validity.

Customer accounts

Maintain customer ledgers with balances, payments and activity charges.

Menu & purchases

Manage daily menu items and record purchases with quantity, amount, supplier and date.

Local data center

Export and import complete application data while keeping the primary records on the device.
