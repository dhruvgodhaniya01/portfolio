PROJECT OVERVIEW

A digital experience for a real café brand.

The live Tokicha site presents the café as a warm destination for tea, coffee, food and moments. The homepage communicates the brand mood, menu highlights, experience, Rajkot location and enquiry path. The live project currently includes sections for menu, story, atmosphere, signature drinks, location, gallery and franchise enquiry. 

HIGHLIGHTS

What the project demonstrates

Brand-first visual system

Warm typography, imagery, spacing and composition keep the café identity at the centre of the experience.

Menu storytelling

Tea, coffee and cooler highlights are presented as visual content instead of a plain list.

Location & discovery

Rajkot location, directions and WhatsApp contact are surfaced as practical conversion paths.

Responsive experience

The interface is structured for modern desktop and mobile browsing.
